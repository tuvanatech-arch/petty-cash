Your cash records shouldn’t depend on who remembers what.

When requests, receipts and cash counts sit in different places, closing the month becomes a search for missing details.

Petti brings the process together, so finance can see what was requested, what was paid and what still needs checking.

Petti can read the shop, date and amounts from a receipt photo to save typing. The person submitting checks the lines, and finance confirms the receipt.

Reconcile

Compare the physical cash count with the recorded balance, review differences and prepare the month-end records.

In the exampleOn 18 November finance counts N$3,983.00, which matches the recorded balance after this payout, and the approver signs the count off. A later count is N$20.00 short: finance gives a reason and it waits for the approver’s review.

Know what is paid, outstanding or missing.

See who requested, approved, paid and checked a transaction. Review outstanding receipts, cash advances and differences without piecing the history together from separate messages and documents.

Follow the transaction history.

Every request keeps its own record: who asked, who approved it and under which approval rules, who paid out, what was spent and who confirmed the receipt. Nobody approves or pays out their own request.

See the example history

See what still needs attention.

Each person’s Home lists the work that is theirs. For finance that means payouts waiting, receipts to check and paper receipts still to come in.

  • A receipt photo already used on another request is marked as a possible duplicate, and accepting it needs an explanation.
  • Spending without a receipt needs a reason from the person who spent it, and finance decides whether to accept it.
  • A cash count that differs from the recorded balance needs a reason from finance and a decision from the approver, who can write the difference off.

Prepare the records for month-end.

When finance closes the month, Petti builds a PDF pack for the accountant. It includes:

  • the reconciliation and every cash count, with its sign-off
  • the petty cash ledger and a monthly summary
  • a voucher for each payout, with its receipts
  • the approval rules that were in force

The same records can be downloaded as spreadsheet files (CSV) with the receipt images, along with a journal file for your accountant.

Differences are not corrected automatically. Petti shows them and records how each one was resolved.

Keep everyday office supplies in the same workflow.

Record deliveries and items issued, review low-stock items and use recorded usage to plan your next restock.

Give the person responsible for supplies a clearer picture of what needs attention.

How a restock moves through Petti

  1. Someone reports that the toilet paper is running low, from their phone.
  2. The person responsible for supplies adds it to a restock order. Petti can draft the order from recorded usage, and they adjust the quantities.
  3. The order follows your approval limits, and finance pays it out like any other request.
  4. The purchase is recorded against the items bought. For larger purchases, someone other than the buyer confirms what arrived, and any shortfall is recorded as a correction.

Questions

Who is Petti for?

Finance and office teams responsible for petty cash, receipts and everyday office supplies. The people who ask for cash, the people who approve it and your accountant all use the same records.

How are plans priced?

One monthly subscription for your company, based on how many of your employees are registered in Petti and how many branches you run (see Pricing). Prices include VAT. When your plan is full, you upgrade to add more people or branches.

What happens after I request a trial?

We contact you on the phone number or email address you give us. We agree your people, approval limits, branches and stock catalogue, set up your company’s own Petti, and send each person their sign-in details.

When does the trial start, and how long does it last?

It begins after setup, once your team can sign in, not when you send the form. It lasts 30 days, is free, and includes your first month-end close. Billing starts on day 31.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes: a once-off N$2,500 for setting up your company’s Petti with your people, approval limits, branches and catalogue. It is charged on your first invoice, after the free trial.

Can we use Petti across multiple branches?

Yes. Each branch has its own petty cash box, cash counts and store. Staff work in their own branch, and the people you choose, such as your approvers, can see every branch. The Business plan covers up to three branches. For more, see the Group plan.

Does everyone need to install an app?

No. Petti runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers, so there is nothing to download from an app store. On a phone you can add it to the Home Screen so it opens like an app. On an iPhone, alerts to the phone only work once Petti has been added to the Home Screen.

What works without an internet connection?

Petti needs a connection for most work: requests, approvals, payouts and receipts are saved on the server. Quick stock counts can be done without signal and are sent once the connection is back. If Petti closes while someone is filling in a form, what they typed is kept on that phone and offered again when it opens.

What happens when a receipt is missing or the cash count differs?

Both are treated as exceptions to resolve, not hidden. Someone without a receipt says why, and finance decides whether to accept it. The spending stays listed as without a receipt. If a cash count differs from the recorded balance, finance gives a reason, and the approver reviews it and decides whether to write the difference off.

How can we export our records?

Finance can download each month’s records as a PDF pack (reconciliation, cash counts, ledger, vouchers and receipts), as spreadsheet files (CSV) with the receipt images, and as a journal file for your accountant.

Is there training?

Guidance is built into Petti. Each person sees how their part works on their own screen, and every update comes with a short walk-through of what changed for them.

How do we cancel, and what happens to our records?

Your administrator cancels in Petti. Before it is confirmed, Petti asks you to download all your records in one file. Nothing more is billed, Petti stays open for 30 days, and you can change your mind within them. After 30 days we remove your records, receipts and backup copies for good.

Can Petti use our currency and our logo?

Yes. Your currency symbol and number format are set once and used on every screen and document. Your company name and logo appear on the month-end pack and the payout documents.
