Your cash records shouldn’t depend on who remembers what.

When requests, receipts and cash counts sit in different places, closing the month becomes a search for missing details.

Petti brings the process together, so finance can see what was requested, what was paid and what still needs checking.

A clear process, from request to reconciliation.

Every request follows the same four steps, and each person sees only the work that is theirs.

  1. 1

    Request

    Ask for cash, a payment to a supplier, or money back for something already paid. A photo of the quote, invoice or receipt fills in most of the form.

  2. 2

    Approve

    Petti sends it to the right person by amount, under the limits your company sets. Nobody approves their own.

  3. 3

    Pay and record

    Finance pays in cash or by bank transfer. The receipt and any change are recorded against the request.

  4. 4

    Reconcile

    Cash counts are set against the records, differences are reviewed, and the month closes into a pack for your accountant.

Built for how petty cash really works.

  • Claims and pay-back dates

    Someone paid from their own pocket? They claim it back with the receipt, and finance can agree a date to pay it.

  • Cash that fits the errand

    Petti suggests how much to hand out from what that supplier charged before, and one person can buy for several requests.

  • Pay suppliers by transfer

    From the petty cash bank account or the company account, with the proof of payment filed on the request.

  • Urgent requests first

    Marked urgent with a reason, they go to the top of each list, and Petti reminds whoever has to act.

  • Works without signal

    People can still see their requests and capture new ones and receipts; Petti sends them when the signal is back.

  • Office supplies too

    Low-stock reports, restock orders, deliveries and what is used each month, in the same app.
