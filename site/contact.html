Start with your team’s working process.

We help configure your people, approval limits and stock catalogue, so you can try Petti using your own working process.

  1. 1

    Request your trial.

    Send the short form above. We contact you to talk through how your petty cash works today.

  2. 2

    Confirm your setup.

    We create your company’s own Petti and load what you give us. You check the approval limits before anyone uses it.

  3. 3

    Try Petti with your team.

    Your team signs in on their own phones or computers. Your 30-day trial begins once setup is done.

What you give us

  • The names, email addresses and roles of the people who will use Petti
  • Your approval limits: what finance may pay alone, and what needs an approver
  • Your branches and the usual monthly petty cash amount for each
  • Your list of stock items, if you want to manage supplies
  • Your logo, if you want it on your documents

What we do

  • Set up a separate database for your company
  • Add your people, approval limits, branches and catalogue
  • Set your currency, your company name and your document numbering
  • Send each person their sign-in details
