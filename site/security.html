How Petti keeps your records safe

Petty cash is money, so we treat its records like money. Here is where your data lives, who can see it, and how signing in is protected.

  • Where your records live

    In a database run by Supabase in Paris, France, inside the European Union. Supabase holds a SOC 2 Type 2 report: an independent audit of its security controls. Every connection between your phone or computer, the app and the database is encrypted (HTTPS).

  • Your own database

    Each company gets its own database and its own app settings. Your records are never mixed with another company's.

  • Who sees what

    Each person sees only what their position needs. Staff see their own requests, finance sees the cash box, and the GM and the MD see everything. These rules are enforced inside the database, not only on the screen.

  • Signing in

    Everyone has their own account and password. Finance, the GM and the MD must also use two-step sign-in: a 6-digit code from their phone after the password, so a stolen password alone cannot release money. Every sign-in is logged with its device, any device can be signed out from another, and someone who leaves is switched off in one tap.

  • A name on every step

    Who asked, who approved, who paid and who checked: every step carries a name and a time. Nobody approves, pays out or confirms their own spending.

  • Copies and leaving

    Petti copies your petty cash and stock records every night. At any month end you can download everything: the PDF pack, plus spreadsheet files for your accountant. If you stop using Petti, you keep a full export.
