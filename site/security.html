How Petti handles your data

An overview of where information is stored, how access is managed and which services support Petti.

Data storage and processing

Each company that uses Petti gets its own database and its own file storage. Your records are not stored in a shared database with other companies’ records.

The database, receipt images and other stored files are hosted by Supabase in its Paris region (France, in the European Union). Supabase holds a SOC 2 Type 2 report. That report covers Supabase’s own controls, not Petti.

Connections between your device, the app and the database use HTTPS. Not every processing step happens in Paris: receipt reading, phone alerts and the web pages themselves are handled by the other services listed under Third-party services.

User access and authentication

  • Everyone signs in with their own account. Accounts are created by your administrator; there is no public sign-up.
  • What each person can see and do depends on their role, for example staff, finance or approver. These rules are enforced in the database, not only hidden on the screen.
  • Your company can require two-step sign-in (a code from an authenticator app after the password) for the roles that handle money, such as finance and approvers.
  • Sign-ins are recorded with the device used. A person can sign out their other devices, and an administrator can switch off someone’s access.
  • Requests record who asked, who approved, who paid out and who confirmed the receipt, with the time of each step. Nobody approves or pays out their own request.

Receipts and AI-assisted processing

When someone adds a receipt photo or PDF, Petti can send that file to Anthropic’s API (the Claude model) to read the shop, date and amounts. The result fills in the form for the person to check. Finance then confirms the receipt. If reading is unavailable, the receipt is checked by eye.

Only the receipt file being read is sent for reading, not your other records. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, Anthropic does not train its models on this data by default. How long Anthropic keeps API data, and where it is processed, is set out in Anthropic’s own terms and privacy documentation. That is a separate question from model training.

Backups and recovery

  • Records: every night, Petti copies your petty cash and stock records to private storage within your Petti project.
  • A second copy elsewhere: every night, those records, together with new or changed receipt images, month-end packs and your logo, are also copied to a private, encrypted storage bucket at a second provider, Backblaze, in its EU Central region (Amsterdam, the Netherlands). The key Petti uses can add files there but cannot read or remove them.
  • Restore check: each night, the records sent to Backblaze are loaded back into a scratch copy of every table to check that they can be restored.
  • Monitoring: each working morning, an automatic check reports a missing or incomplete copy to Tuvana Tech.

These copies make recovery possible. No recovery time or recovery point is promised here.

Data export and deletion

Finance can download each month’s records at any time: a PDF month-end pack (reconciliation, cash counts, ledger, vouchers and receipts), spreadsheet files (CSV) with the receipt images, and a journal file for your accountant.

To ask about deleting your company’s data, contact us as described under Contact and security questions.
